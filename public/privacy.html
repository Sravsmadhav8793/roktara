Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy covers Roktara (com.sravantix.roktara) and its web service at roktara.sravantix.com. Sravantix operates this private, sign-in protected service.

Information processed

We process the authorized account email, password verification data, Google account identity when Google sign-in is chosen, passkey public keys and counters when a device passkey is registered, session and security logs, and the following app data: Sign-in identifiers, passkey public keys, and security logs. The current workspace displays sample fraud cases and risk checks; do not enter real payment PINs, one-time passwords, or full account numbers.

Uses and sharing

We use this information to authenticate access, provide requested app functions, maintain the service, prevent abuse, and respond to support or legal requests. Hosting and database providers process service data on our behalf. Google receives sign-in requests when you choose Google sign-in. We do not sell account or app data for advertising.

Security and retention

Connections use HTTPS. Session cookies are secured, and passkeys use device verification. We retain account and app records while needed to run the service and for applicable recordkeeping, then delete or de-identify them where permitted. Backups and security logs may persist for a limited operational period after deletion.

Your choices

You may remove a registered passkey in Device sign-in settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of an account and associated personal data, use the account deletion request page. We verify requests before acting and explain any records that must be retained.
